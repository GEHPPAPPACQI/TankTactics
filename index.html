Rules

  • 15×15 board, 3 lives, start with 1 AP.
  • Actions resolve instantly. Every day at 12:00 all living tanks gain +1 AP.
  • Move — 1 AP, one cell (8 directions; not into water, tanks or borders).
  • Attack — 1 AP, circular range 3, deals 1 damage.
  • Transfer — give any amount of AP to another living tank.
  • Defend — 1 AP, blocks 1 damage. Max one defense per day, even with more AP.
  • Fog of war: you see a circle of radius 6 around your tank. Attack range is separate (3).
  • You may queue actions only up to your available AP.
  • Dead tanks learn only who killed them, and vanish from the board.
